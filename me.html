Experiences Aug 8 - 12, 2023:  Hug Kor Chiang Khan Short Film Camp We, Front Row Group, which consisted of me, Thaninnat Chotikonpong, Sittinee Thepnakorn, Natchaya Songthong, and Jirapas Kliengkerd, got a chance to be the representative team to enter this camp in the name of Khon Kaen University International College. In the camp, we got to meet the top-tier directors from both Thailand and Laos and hear a lecture from them, which is almost a once-in-a-lifetime thing. Along with 30 other teams across Thailand and Laos, we got to write a script and shoot a short film within 3 days, which is somehow stressful, but it's an opportunity to try something new and test my limits.
The comments and suggestions from the directors were something we learned a lot from. I'm definitely sure that the experience and knowledge gained from this camp will be beneficial in the future.
The final product for our group is a horror short film called "The Mouth (ปากเป็น)," which is the Isan word for talking without thinking about the consequence or talkative but nonsense. This word can describe all the story in the short film perfectly, since the main theme of the short film is the main character, who doesn't believe in any ghosts or holy things, then gets into a story that can result in her life from the holy things from her own mouth.
