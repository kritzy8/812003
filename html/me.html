The Beauty of Mortality: Embalming Artistry
May 15, 2024
This documentary is the final project for the Documentary Film and Video class. In this project, I'm responsible as the co-director, co-videographer, co-coordinator, video editor, and co-translator. The final project's topic is up to the students to come up with freely and with the supervision of the professor. During the brainstorming process, one of the Front Row Group members, Pinnudda Thiyathat, found a picture of her grandfather when he passed away two years ago. She remembered that at that time, there was an embalming artist who did the make-up for her grandfather after he passed away. After consulting with the professor and receiving good feedback, that became our group topic for the final project of this class.
The preparation process for this project required us to contact many organizations in the Esan region of Thailand. This included the embalming artist (Ms. Sarinya Utaisa), Srinagarind Hospital, and KKU Academy. Which is very tough when the time to do the project is two weeks and a half. But at the end, we did it and learned so much from this project in terms of how to contact big organizations and the importance of the embalming artist career. It's a very special occasion to have a chance to film in a big hospital like Srinagarind Hospital and have an embalming artist do the make-up for the real dead body in front of our eyes. This might be a once-in-a-lifetime experience. We're quite fascinated and fun to do all of this, and this ended up being one of the proudest of Front Row Group.
SHEOUT application mockup and brand identity
Apr 11, 2024
This is part of the final project for the Advanced Mutimedia Production class, where we, Front Row Group, are assigned to do an application that proposes to fight the fast fashion crisis in Gen Z. In this project, I'm responsible as a co-designer for both the application and brand identity.
As Gen Z, we know that one of the problems with fast fashion is that it is unstoppable for Gen Z to stop consuming new fashion. So, to prevent the old clothes and accessories from becoming a waste that harms the planet, we created SHEOUT. SHEOUT is a second-hand clothes and accessories sell and buy platform that aims to reduce waste from the unwanted clothes and accessories of Gen Z and other groups of people. To give those clothes and accessories a second or third life and save the world at the same time. The design of the SHEOUT application and brand identity are very modern and easy to use, matching the needs of Gen Z. The logo is one of my most proudest works on this project. I love the meaning behind it that I put into it. The word OUT with the crossout line symbolizes that there won't be any clothes or accessories left out and become a waste. This is a very genius idea of mine.
